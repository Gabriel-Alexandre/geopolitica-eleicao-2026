China congratulates Brazilian president Lula on his presidential election victory: spokesperson
Politics
17:43, 26-Oct-2026
CGTN
Share
Copied
Brazilian incumbent President Luiz Inacio Lula da Silva (C), candidate of the ruling Workers' Party, delivers a speech following his victory in the presidential runoff in Sao Paulo, Brazil, October 25, 2026. /Xinhua
Brazilian incumbent President Luiz Inacio Lula da Silva (C), candidate of the ruling Workers' Party, delivers a speech following his victory in the presidential runoff in Sao Paulo, Brazil, October 25, 2026. /Xinhua
China has noted that Brazil's Superior Electoral Court announced President Luiz Inacio Lula da Silva's victory in the second round of Brazil's presidential election, and extends its congratulations, Chinese Foreign Ministry spokesperson Guo Jiakun said at a regular press briefing on Monday.
China stands ready to work with Brazil to steadily advance the building of the China-Brazil community with a shared future, Guo added
