‘Congratulations!’: World leaders hail Lula’s election victory in Brazil
The international community on Monday congratulated Brazil's President Luiz Inacio Lula da Silva after his stunning re-election in a runoff with right-wing candidate Flavio Bolsonaro. The region's left-leaning leaders were quick to react as Brazil staved off a conservative wave in many countries while Europeans, such as Spanish Prime Minister Pedro Sanchez, celebrated a "progressive comeback".

Here are some reactions from leaders across the world:
Macron calls for France and Brazil to work for 'a fairer world'
French President
Emmanuel Macron
on Monday congratulated his Brazilian counterpart and calling for efforts to "strengthen our strategic partnership".
"France and Brazil have much to build together," Macron said X, saying their two countries should nurture their friendship "in the service of a fairer world".
French President Emmanuel Macron on Monday congratulated his Brazilian counterpart Luiz Inacio Lula da Silva in a post on X.        © @EmmanuelMacron, X
Spain's Sanchez hails 'global progressive comeback'
Spanish Prime Minister Pedro Sanchez celebrated his leftist Brazilian ally's re-election victory, calling it a "progressive comeback", as he trails in polls before Spain's November vote.
"A great victory for Lula! What has happened in Brazil will happen in Spain. The global progressive comeback has just begun," Sanchez posted on X on Monday, after Lula's recovery from a first-round loss halted a string of right-wing gains in Latin America.
Spanish Prime Minister Pedro Sanchez on Monday celebrated his leftist Brazilian ally Luiz Inacio Lula da Silva's re-election victory.        © @sanchezcastejon, X
China 'expresses its congratulations'
China on Monday congratulated Lula on his re-election, saying it was willing to continue working together.
"China has noted that Brazil's Superior Electoral Court has announced President Lula has won the second round of the Brazilian general election, and expresses its congratulations," foreign ministry spokesperson Guo Jiakun said at a regular briefing, adding that Beijing was "willing to work with Brazil to continue advancing the building of a China-Brazil community with a shared future".
Asked about Bolsonaro saying he would request an annulment of the elections, Guo said: "China respects the choice of the Brazilian people."
Kremlin welcomes Lula win
The Kremlin on Monday welcomed Lula's re-election victory, saying he had done "a lot" to develop ties with Russia.
"We welcome Mr. Lula's victory. He does a lot, not only for the development of bilateral relations but also for advancing processes within the framework of BRICS," Kremlin spokesman Dmitry Peskov told reporters, referring to the international bloc of which both countries are members.
EU chief hails Lula's Brazil victory as win for 'democracy'
EU chief
Ursula von der Leyen
on Monday hailed Lula's re-election victory as a win for "democracy and multilateralism".
She congratulated Lula on social media, adding: "Europe's partnership with Brazil is thriving. We will remain by your side for the years to come. Together we will keep working closely for free and open trade. A rules-based global order. And strong ambitions for our climate."
'Long live democracy': Mexico congratulates Lula
Mexico's left-wing President
Claudia Sheinbaum
congratulated her Brazilian counterpart Lula on winning Sunday's presidential runoff, staving off a conservative wave seen elsewhere throughout Latin America.
"Long live democracy, the people of Brazil won. Congratulations, President Lula!" Sheinbaum wrote on X.
Peru affirms strategic alliance
Peru's right-wing President Keiko Fujimori praised Brazil's "democratic day" of elections and congratulated Lula on his win.
"I reaffirm our willingness to strengthen our strategic alliance, integration, cooperation, and the sustainable development of the Amazon that we share, for the benefit of both our peoples," she said on X.
Cuba welcomes 'historic victory'
Cuba
hailed Lula's re-election as a "historic victory that ensures the continuity of the path of peace, sovereignty and social justice embarked upon by the Brazilian people under your leadership".
In a congratulatory statement, Havana said, "Brazil can count on Cuba in the construction of the unity and integration of our Great Homeland, Latin American and the Caribbean."
India congratulates a BRICS partner
Indian Prime Minister
Narendra Modi
, who is the current chair of the
BRICS
geopolitical and economic bloc, which includes Brazil, offered Lula his "heartiest congratulations".
"I look forward to working closely with him to further deepen and expand the multifaceted India-Brazil Strategic Partnership," he added.
