Elecciones generales del 29 de noviembre · Demócrata
The Chinese Executive has conveyed this Monday its congratulations to the president of Brazil, Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva, after winning in the second round of the elections held in the South American country, and has expressed its willingness to deepen cooperation and bilateral ties between both nations.
"China congratulates Lula on his victory in the second round of the presidential elections and is ready to work with Brazil to continue advancing in the construction of a community between China and Brazil with a shared future," said the spokesperson of the Chinese Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Guo Jiakun.
When asked about the statements of Lula's rival, the far-right Flávio Bolsonaro, who has rejected the results, the representative of the Ministry avoided commenting on these accusations and limited himself to emphasizing that Beijing "respects the choice of the Brazilian people," according to the Chinese newspaper 'Global Times'.
According to the data released by the electoral commission, Lula has achieved 51.77% of the valid votes, while the son of former president Jair Bolsonaro has gathered 48.23% of the votes, with a count of 99.89% and a turnout of around 80.8%.
