Congratulations to Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva on his victory in Brazil’s presidential election
Vladimir Putin sent a message of congratulations to Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva on his victory in Brazil’s presidential election.
October 26, 2026
12:40
The message reads, in part:
“The election results clearly
demonstrate that your fellow citizens recognise your outstanding leadership
qualities and support your policy of ensuring the country’s sustainable
socioeconomic development and defending its sovereignty and legitimate
interests on the international stage.
Your personal contribution to strengthening the strategic partnership between Brazil and Russia can hardly be
overstated. We will certainly continue to work closely together to further
develop fruitful bilateral cooperation across all areas and maintain
constructive interaction within the UN, BRICS, the G20 and other multilateral platforms.
I would also like to extend my heartfelt birthday
wishes and wish you good health, happiness, prosperity and continued success in your work for the benefit of the friendly Brazilian people.”
